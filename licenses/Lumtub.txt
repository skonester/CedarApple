Ported from the supplied Lumtub 0.2.36 source:

- src/lumtub/listing.rs: native/lumtub_core/src/listing.rs, including its tests.
  The Dart FFI wrapper was removed and imports were adapted.
- src/lumtub/innertube.rs: the at, seconds and members_only helpers from
  native/lumtub_core/src/innertube.rs, with checked duration arithmetic.
- src/lumtub/catalog.rs: requests, client headers and filter parameters from
  lib/youtube_listing.dart, lib/youtube_web.dart and lib/video_repository.dart.
- src/lumtub/tools.rs: the EDL stream construction from
  native/lumtub_core/src/resolved.rs; the tool preparation and stream resolver
  follow lib/tool_manager.dart and lib/stream_resolver.dart.

CedarApple's controller, local storage and Slint interface are adaptations
for the native host. Flutter, PiliNara UI, fonts and skins are not included.

yt-dlp and Deno are optional runtime tools downloaded from their upstream
GitHub releases on first playback, or discovered beside CedarApple or on PATH.
They are not distributed in CedarApple's source or release archives.
